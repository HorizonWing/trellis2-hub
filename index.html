Trellis 2

AI image-to-3D generator on Microsoft SLAT — single image to GLB in 1-3 minutes, cloud-based, no GPU required

Trellis 2 is an AI image-to-3D generator built on Microsoft Research's SLAT architecture (2B parameters, trained on 500K+ 3D objects). Upload a single image and get a previewable 3D model in 1-3 minutes, exportable as GLB for Blender, Unity and Unreal. Runs fully in the cloud — no GPU required. Credit-based plans from $19/mo.
